Frequently asked

Is there a free trial?
Yes — all plans include a free trial period with no credit card required. You can run real settlements and generate reports before committing.
How does per-worker billing work?
The Combo and Payroll plans are priced per active worker per month. A worker is counted as active if they had at least one settlement run in the billing period. Bookkeeping-only plans are flat-rate with no per-worker charge.
Can I change plans later?
Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your Stripe-powered billing portal. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe. ACH bank transfers are available on annual plans. All payments are processed securely through Stripe — we never store your card details.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Our database runs on managed PostgreSQL with automated backups. Access is scoped by role — drivers, clients, and admins each see only what they're authorized to see.
Do you support owner-operators and fleets?
Yes — LedgerHaul handles both single-driver owner-operators and multi-driver fleets. Pay structures can be configured per driver. There's no minimum fleet size.
