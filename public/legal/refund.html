Refund Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. General Policy

All subscription fees paid to LedgerHaul are non-refundable unless otherwise stated in this policy or required by applicable law. By subscribing, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Free Trial

All plans include a free trial period. You will not be charged until your trial ends. You may cancel at any time before your trial expires to avoid any charges.

3. Eligible Refunds

You may be eligible for a refund in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate charges: If you were charged multiple times for the same billing period, we will refund the duplicate charge.
  • Service outage: If the Service was unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours due to our fault, you may request a pro-rated credit.
  • Within 7 days of first charge: New customers who request a refund within 7 days of their first subscription payment may receive a full refund, at our discretion.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at billing@ledgerhaul.com within 14 days of the charge. Include:

  • Your account email address
  • The date and amount of the charge
  • The reason for the refund request

We will respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days.

5. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time through the billing portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You will not be charged for the next period, but no refund is provided for the unused portion of the current period.

6. Contact

For billing questions, contact us at billing@ledgerhaul.com.
