Summary: LedgerHaul is a subscription software service. Refund eligibility depends on billing type, timing, and account status as described below.

1) Subscription Fees

Subscription fees are charged in advance for the billing period shown at checkout (monthly or annual, if offered). Unless required by law, subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period begins.

2) Trials and First-Time Subscriptions

  • If a free trial is offered, you may cancel before the trial ends to avoid charges.
  • If you are charged due to forgetting to cancel before trial end, refunds are not guaranteed.

3) Upgrade / Downgrade

  • Upgrades may take effect immediately. Any proration is handled by the payment processor (Stripe) based on your plan configuration.
  • Downgrades generally take effect at the next renewal unless stated otherwise during plan change.

4) Cancellation

You may cancel at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals, but does not automatically refund fees already paid. Your access remains active through the end of the current paid period unless your account is suspended for policy violations.

5) Payment Disputes and Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute, we may suspend service access while the dispute is investigated. Repeated disputes or fraud indicators may result in account termination.

6) Exceptional Refunds (Case-by-Case)

We may consider a refund in limited cases, for example:

  • Duplicate charges confirmed on the same billing period.
  • Billing errors caused by a system malfunction verified by our logs.
  • Other scenarios required by applicable law.

Any approved refund is issued to the original payment method and may take several business days to post depending on your bank.

7) Taxes and Third-Party Fees

Taxes, governmental fees, and third-party processing fees are generally non-refundable unless required by law.

8) How to Request a Refund Review

To request a refund review, contact support and include:

  • Account email
  • Company name (if applicable)
  • Invoice ID or Stripe receipt reference
  • Reason for request

This Refund Policy is provided for transparency and operational clarity. It does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.
