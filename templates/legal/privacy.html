Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

FreightPay (“Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access or use the FreightPay platform (the “Platform” or “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (name, email, company details)
  • Billing and payment data processed via Stripe
  • Payroll, bookkeeping, and operational data you submit
  • Usage, audit, and system logs for compliance and security
  • Support communications and requests you submit through the Service
  • Device and technical information (e.g., IP address, browser type, timestamps) used for security and fraud prevention

1.1 Sensitive Personal Data

Certain information processed through the Service may be considered sensitive or highly confidential, including but not limited to: tax identifiers (e.g., SSN, EIN), wage and compensation data, payroll records, worker classification information, bank account details, and other financial account information. We process such data solely to provide, secure, and support the Service and to comply with law.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and operate the FreightPay services
  • To process billing, subscriptions, and payments
  • To enable integrations you request (e.g., payroll partners such as Gusto, if enabled)
  • To comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations
  • To secure, audit, monitor, and improve platform performance
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To communicate with you about service updates, security notices, and support matters

2.1 No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only with:

  • Payment processors (e.g., Stripe)
  • Payroll and compliance partners (e.g., Gusto, if enabled)
  • Infrastructure and service providers that support hosting, security, communications, and system operations
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law, subpoena, court order, or valid legal process
  • Professional advisors (e.g., legal, accounting) under confidentiality obligations when necessary to operate the business

3.1 Sub-Processors

We may use third-party service providers (“Sub-processors”) to process data on our behalf for the purpose of providing the Service. Sub-processors may include categories such as payment processing, payroll providers, cloud hosting, logging/monitoring, email/SMS delivery, and customer support tooling. Sub-processors are authorized to access and process data only as necessary to perform services for us and are expected to protect data consistent with this Policy and applicable contractual obligations.

3.2 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and appropriate confidentiality protections.

4. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational safeguards including access controls, encryption, audit logging, and environment isolation to protect your data.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4.1 Incident Response

In the event of a data security incident involving personal data, we will take reasonable steps to investigate, mitigate, and remediate the incident. Where required by applicable law, we will provide notification to affected parties and/or regulators.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for operational, legal, and regulatory purposes. Audit and financial records may be retained to meet compliance requirements.

Upon account termination, data deletion requests may be honored subject to applicable legal retention requirements, dispute resolution needs, fraud prevention, and technical limitations. Certain records (e.g., billing, audit, or compliance logs) may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and contractual obligations.

Requests must be submitted through the Service or via the contact method below. We may need to verify your identity and your authority to make the request before responding.

6.1 Customer Role (Controller/Processor)

When you use the Service for payroll, bookkeeping, or business operations, you may submit information about employees, contractors, or other individuals. In such cases, you are responsible for providing any required notices and obtaining any required consents. For certain customer-submitted data, the Customer may act as the “controller” and FreightPay may act as a “processor” to provide the Service.

7. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed and stored in jurisdictions different from your own. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data protection requirements.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@freightpay.app.
